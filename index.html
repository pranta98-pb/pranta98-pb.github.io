About Me

I am a geoscience researcher with an academic background in Geography and Environment and current MSc studies in Georesources Management at RWTH Aachen University. My work focuses on understanding environmental processes through GIS, remote sensing and spatial modelling.

I enjoy combining geospatial datasets, analytical methods and scientific research to investigate environmental challenges and support evidence-based decision-making.

Research Interests

GIS & Remote Sensing

Spatial analysis, satellite imagery, land use and land cover change, and environmental monitoring.

Hydrogeology

Flood Risk Assessment

Flood hazard and vulnerability mapping using GIS, AHP and machine learning approaches.

Environmental Quality
